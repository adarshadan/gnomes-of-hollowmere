      How to play

      Race your gnome from the Burrow on square 1 to the Summit Hearth on square 100. The first gnome to land exactly on 100 wins.

      🎲Your turn

      When your card glows, it is your turn. Throw the die in any of these ways:

      1. Roll dice button, or press Space or Enter.
      2. Press and wiggle the die icon, or wiggle the mouse over it, then let go.
      3. Shake your phone (tap Enable phone shake first; needs the game opened from a secure web link).

      The die lands with the result on top, the sum is shown, and your gnome walks that many squares.

      ⚖️Difficulty and dice

        🪜Vine ladders

        End your move on the foot of a ladder and you climb straight to its top.

        🐉Monster lairs

        End your move on a lair and the monster attacks, hurling you back to the square its dotted trail points to.

        The Nearby chips on your card show monsters within one throw. Tap a chip to spot it on the board.

        💎Relics

        Land on a glowing crystal to collect its relic. Your satchel holds four.

        A relic that can be used now glows in your card: tap it before you roll. Gale Feather acts at once and Oakheart Ward blocks the next attack by itself. Collected relics reappear elsewhere later.

        With a full satchel you can swap one of your relics for the new one, or leave the new one where it lies. The relic left behind stays on that square for any gnome to claim.

        🔥The summit

        Within six squares of 100 you throw the golden Hearthstone die (1 to 6). You need the exact number; a throw that would pass 100 leaves you where you are.

        🧭Finding your way

        • Each gnome stands in a beam of light in its colour. The gnome whose turn it is has the brightest beam.
        • Hover over a square (with a mouse) to see what is on it.
        • The camera follows each gnome as it moves; turn this off with Follow in the toolbar.
        • The Chronicle lists every move. Use Reset view if you lose the board, or drag to look around.
        • In a rounds match, the highest gnome when the rounds run out wins, unless someone reaches 100 first.
        • Your game saves itself. Choose Continue saved game on the title screen to carry on.
